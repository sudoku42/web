Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

sudoku42 stores an anonymous account ID and the game data needed to run the app: game IDs, moves, notes, puzzle difficulty, and timelines. This lets you play, sync, and view your games. This data is stored with Google Firebase.

If you join a multiplayer game, the app can send notifications about activity in that game using Firebase Cloud Messaging.

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner and rewarded ads. Google may collect information about your device and ad interactions to show and measure ads. You can read more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s AdMob information for app users.
